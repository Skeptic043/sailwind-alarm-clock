## Version 0.2.0

### Added

- Alarm added to all chronometers.
- Hold `Z` while holding or looking at a chronometer and scroll to set the time in 15-minute steps. Press R with `Z` held to switch the alarm on or off. A short message confirms each change.
- A ringing chronometer wakes you from sleep, whether you are in a bed or sleeping aboard at sea. Look at it or hold it to see "stop alarm" beside the R icon, and press R to stop it.
- Alarms ring only when you are near the chronometer, and stop by themselves after a few seconds.
- Alarms stay silent while you rest at an inn, and ring as usual the next day.
- Alarm times and on/off states are saved with your game.
- Settings for the modifier key, ring range, ring duration, volume and bell pitch.
