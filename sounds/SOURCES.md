# Alarm sound sources

The recorded alarm sounds are trimmed, looped and level-matched from these recordings by Joseph Sardin, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain) on BigSoundBank.

- `warm-clock-loop.wav` and `warm-clock-tail.wav`: [Mechanical alarm clock, short ring](https://bigsoundbank.com/mechanical-alarm-clock-short-ring-s1373.htm)
- `bright-clock-loop.wav` and `bright-clock-tail.wav`: [Mechanical alarm clock, ringtone #11](https://bigsoundbank.com/mechanical-alarm-clock-ringtone-11-s2814.htm)

The original Sailwind bell is the game's own sound and is not included.
